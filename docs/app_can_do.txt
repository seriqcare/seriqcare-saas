What Your App Can Do
1. Collect and Process EVV Visit Data
Your app can fully manage the EVV workflow on behalf of caregivers and agencies. This includes:

Capturing clock‑in and clock‑out times

Recording GPS location (if required by the state)

Logging tasks performed during the visit

Identifying the caregiver and client

Validating service codes before submission

Storing visit history and audit trails

This forms the core of your EVV system and ensures that all required data elements are captured accurately.

2. Submit Visits to HHAeXchange via the Alternate EVV API
Your app can integrate directly with HHAeXchange using the official Alternate EVV API. This allows you to:

Send completed visits in real time

Receive immediate accept/reject responses

Store HHAX validation results

Resubmit corrected visits

Track visit status across the entire lifecycle

This ensures agencies remain compliant with state EVV requirements.

3. Track Visit Status and Provide Operational Visibility
Your app can give agencies a clear view of visit outcomes, including:

Pending visits

Successfully submitted visits

Accepted visits

Rejected visits with error reasons

Corrected and resubmitted visits

This is something agencies struggle to see clearly in HHAeXchange, so your app adds real value.

4. Process V5 Billing Files Uploaded by the Agency
Your app can process V5 files if the agency downloads them from HHAeXchange and uploads them to your system. With these files, your app can:

Billing Exceptions V5
Show why visits failed billing

Link exceptions to the original EVV visit

Provide context (GPS, tasks, caregiver)

Suggest corrections

Processed Visits V5
Display which visits were billed

Show denied or adjusted visits

Track billed units vs paid units

Claim Response V5
Show payer acceptance or denial

Display denial reason codes

Track claim status

Remittance/Payment V5
Show payment amounts

Display check/EFT numbers

Track paid vs unpaid visits

This transforms your app into a billing reconciliation dashboard, something agencies do not get from HHAX.

5. Provide Analytics and Summaries
Using your EVV data + V5 billing data, your app can generate:

Client billing summaries

Caregiver hours and visit summaries

Service code utilization

Authorization usage (if provided by agency)

Billing vs payment comparisons

This gives agencies insights they cannot easily get from HHAeXchange.

What Your App Cannot Do
1. Your App Cannot Access HHAeXchange Billing Data Directly
HHAeXchange does not give Alternate EVV vendors:

SFTP access

Billing files

Claim files

Remittance files

Authorization feeds

Client or caregiver lists

Only the agency receives these.

Your app can only process V5 files after the agency downloads them and uploads them to your system.

2. Your App Cannot Replace HHAeXchange Billing
HHAeXchange remains the official billing system. Your app cannot:

Submit claims to payers

Generate 837 claim files

Replace HHAX’s billing engine

Modify authorizations

Change payer configurations

Your app supports billing, but does not perform billing.

3. Your App Cannot Create or Modify Data Inside HHAeXchange
Your app cannot:

Create clients

Create caregivers

Create service codes

Create authorizations

Update agency data in HHAX

Only the agency can configure these inside HHAeXchange.

Your app must rely on the agency to provide the correct identifiers.

4. Your App Cannot Automatically Retrieve V5 Files
Because HHAX does not allow EVV vendors to access SFTP:

Your app cannot download V5 files

Your app cannot poll HHAX for billing data

Your app cannot sync billing data automatically

The agency must manually upload the files to your system.

5. Your App Cannot Guarantee Billing Success
Your app can help agencies:

Identify issues

Correct visit data

Understand billing outcomes

But it cannot:

Control payer decisions

Override HHAX billing rules

Fix authorization issues inside HHAX

Prevent denials caused by payer policies

Billing success ultimately depends on the agency’s HHAX setup and payer rules.

Summary
Your app is a powerful EVV and billing‑support platform, but it operates alongside HHAeXchange, not inside it.

Your app can do:
Collect EVV data

Submit visits via API

Track visit status

Process V5 billing files

Provide billing reconciliation

Offer analytics and summaries

Your app cannot do:
Access HHAX billing data directly

Replace HHAX billing

Create or modify HHAX records

Automatically retrieve V5 files

Guarantee payer billing outcomes
